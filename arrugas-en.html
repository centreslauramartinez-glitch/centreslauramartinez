Lines and wrinkles appear through a combination of ageing, progressive collagen loss, sun exposure and other factors. The aim is to improve the skin while preserving expression and naturalness.

Understanding ageing

Time changes the skin.
We understand it.

Lines and wrinkles can appear for different reasons. Over time, collagen production decreases and the skin progressively loses firmness, hydration and its ability to renew.

Factors such as sun exposure, hormonal changes and environmental conditions can add to this process and accelerate visible signs of ageing.

At Laura Martínez Clinic, we work with different minimally invasive options aimed at improving skin quality and addressing signs of ageing in a personalised way, seeking natural and harmonious results.
