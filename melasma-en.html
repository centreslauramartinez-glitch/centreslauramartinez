Melasma is a common condition that appears as brown or greyish patches. It may be related to excessive sun exposure or hormonal changes, such as those associated with pregnancy.

Although it can affect both men and women, it is especially common in women. Before starting any protocol, it is therefore important to assess the pigmentation and the skin as a whole.

At Laura Martínez Clinic, we work with non-invasive treatments aimed at progressively improving the appearance of pigmentation and restoring a more even skin appearance.
