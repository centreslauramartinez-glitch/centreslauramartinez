Understanding the skin

A treatment that
starts with diagnosis.

Acne is a common condition that can affect men and women at any age. In addition to visible lesions, it can leave marks and scars that affect the skin’s texture and appearance.

At Laura Martínez Clinic, we approach acne from a comprehensive perspective, analysing each skin’s characteristics and needs to design a personalised protocol, both to control active acne and improve marks and scars.

An effective treatment begins with an accurate diagnosis. Identifying the type of acne, its progression and contributing factors makes it possible to select the most appropriate combination of technologies and treatments in each case.

Personalised assessment

First, we understand.
Then, we treat.

Not all types of acne require the same treatment. The technology, intensity and combination of procedures are determined by the condition of the skin, the type of acne and each person’s goals.

The choice may include BDR Anti-acne, radiofrequency microneedling, Genesis laser, Line Light, chemical peels and nutricosmetic support, always within an individualised protocol.

First step

During the initial skin diagnosis, we analyse your skin and guide you towards the most appropriate protocol. Treating acne is not only about eliminating lesions, but understanding the skin so it can regain balance, texture and a healthy appearance.
