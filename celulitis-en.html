Understanding the tissue

A treatment that
starts with diagnosis.

Cellulite is a very common alteration of the subcutaneous tissue and can be particularly frustrating. It can appear even when healthy eating and exercise habits are maintained.

At Laura Martínez Clinic, we approach cellulite from a personalised perspective, considering its type and degree of involvement before selecting the technology or procedure.

The aim is to work precisely on the factors that affect the skin’s appearance and adapt the protocol to individual needs.

Treatment options

Technology and treatment
according to your skin.

Laura Martínez Clinic offers different options for addressing cellulite. The choice depends on the type and severity of the concern and is determined during the initial skin diagnosis.

Intralipotene

It introduces collagenase through an injectable treatment directed at the collagen fibres forming the septa. According to the supplied content, it acts by releasing the skin from tissue bridges responsible for certain dimples.

Endymed 3 Deep Shaper

A focused radiofrequency treatment designed to work on cellulite and support collagen production, also improving the appearance of lax skin.

Vinci Liporadiologi

It combines radiofrequency, LED and infrared to work on the skin layers and subcutaneous tissue. The treatment is aimed at reducing fat and improving the appearance of cellulite.

Personalised assessment

First, we understand.

Not all cellulite is the same and not all bodies respond in the same way. Treatment is therefore selected according to each person’s characteristics.

One of the procedures above, or a combination of them, may be more appropriate. The aim is to establish a protocol consistent with individual needs and goals.
