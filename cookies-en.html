Cookie policy

The reviewed website code contains no advertising analytics tags such as Google Analytics or Meta Pixel. The website loads Google Fonts, which may connect to Google servers and disclose technical data such as the IP address.

1. Technical cookies

Hosting and security services may use strictly necessary technical cookies to provide the service, protect the website or balance traffic. These cookies are not used to build advertising profiles.

2. Analytics and advertising cookies

No first-party analytics or advertising cookies have been identified in the reviewed code. If measurement tools, maps, videos, advertising pixels or other services that set non-essential cookies are added in future, they must be blocked until valid consent is obtained.

3. Consent

The Spanish Data Protection Agency requires accepting and rejecting non-essential cookies to be offered at the same level and with equal visibility. If optional cookies are introduced, a settings panel will be added to allow users to accept, reject and withdraw consent.

4. Browser settings

You can configure or delete cookies through your browser settings. This policy must be reviewed if new tools are added or hosting settings change.

5. External forms

When you submit the contact form, the browser communicates with Web3Forms to transmit your request. See the Web3Forms privacy policy for information about its service. Third-party cookies must be verified in the browser and reviewed if new tools are added.
